⛽ Fuel Tracker — Support

Fuel Tracker is an internal tool for logging fuel usage across tanks, trailers, and service stations. Access is by invitation only — contact your administrator to get a join code.


Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a group?
Ask your administrator for the group's 6-character join code. On the welcome screen, tap Join Group, enter the code, then set a display name. You're in — no account or password required.

I entered the join code but it didn't work
Join codes are case-insensitive, but make sure you've entered all 6 characters correctly. If it still fails, ask your administrator to confirm the code — they can generate a new one from Group Settings if needed.

A transaction I logged isn't showing up
If your device was offline when you logged it, the transaction is saved locally and syncs automatically once you're back online. You'll see an orange badge on the dashboard while items are waiting to sync.

How do I attach a receipt photo?
When logging a transaction, scroll down to Receipt Photo and tap Add receipt photo. You can take a new photo with the camera or choose one from your photo library.

What is a meter irregularity?
Fuel Tracker checks the meter readings entered on each withdrawal. If readings go backwards, skip unexpectedly, or don't match the amount withdrawn, the entry is flagged. Admins can review and accept flagged entries from the dashboard.

How do I export transactions?
Admins can export a CSV from Settings → Export Transactions. Filter by tank, vehicle, and date range, then tap Export as CSV to share or save the file.

Can I use the app without internet?
Yes. Transactions logged without an internet connection are saved on your device and automatically uploaded when connectivity is restored.

How do I unlock admin mode?
Tap Unlock Admin Mode in Settings and enter the admin passcode set by the group creator. Admin mode is session-only — it resets when you restart the app.

How do I add a new tank?
Tank management is available to admins (and operators with permission) under Settings → Manage Tanks.

How do I see who's in the group?
Open Settings → Group Settings. The Members section lists everyone who has joined, along with when they joined and who the group creator is.

What is a Service Station tank?
Service station tanks have unlimited supply. The app logs withdrawals and tracks usage but does not deduct from a running level or show a fill percentage.

Features


Group-based access — join with a 6-character code; no account or password needed
Multiple tanks — stationary tanks, fuel trailers, and unlimited service stations
Log withdrawals and refills — with live before/after level preview
Vehicle/equipment linking — associate fuel with a specific piece of equipment
Real-time sync — all group members see live updates
Offline support — transactions queue locally and sync automatically when back online
Transaction history — filterable by tank, grouped by date
Low fuel warnings — tanks below 20% are flagged red
Admin mode — unlock with a passcode to manage tanks, export data, and configure group settings
Member directory — see everyone in the group from Group Settings
